Signed payments, billing line items and split charge and credit statistics.

- Payments keep the retailer's sign. v0.1.1 stored every payment as a positive amount, so a refund paid out looked like a payment received. The payments statistic is re-imported in full on the first refresh.
- Charges and credits are now separate statistics: `_charges` holds gross charges and the new `_credits` holds credits as a positive amount. v0.1.1's `_charges` was net of credits.
- New month-to-date credits sensor. Both month-to-date sensors split their totals by transaction type.
- Diagnostics list every billing line (date, type, amount, GST, and whether it names an invoice or meter) with no descriptions or identifiers, plus totals per money series.
