More energy fields, billing history and a DER register check.

- Peak grid import and export: daily peak sensors and hourly mean, min and max power statistics from the meter's own 5 or 30 minute reads, so short peaks survive hourly aggregation.
- Billing history: billing transactions and invoices imported as money statistics (charges, payments, invoiced), plus a month-to-date charges sensor when the retailer shares billing lines.
- DER register: a solar capacity sensor listing every inverter and device, and a register issues sensor that flags decommissioned records, battery inverters without storage, and capacity above the approved limit.
- Plan: read from the account detail endpoint when the account list omits it. The plan sensor is removed when the retailer shares no plan.
- History depth: the usage sensor and diagnostics show the oldest usage, invoice and billing dates openfeed returned.
- Privacy: diagnostics now also redact invoice meter IDs, equipment identifiers, names and billing descriptions. Added PRIVACY.md.
