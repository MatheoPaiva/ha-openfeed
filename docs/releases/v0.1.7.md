Plan tariff sensors and full meter detail.

- **Plan tariff sensors.** When your retailer shares a plan with tariff detail, new sensors show the import rate in force now (including GST), the time-of-use period, the feed-in rate, the daily supply charge, any demand charge, and the next tariff change. They follow seasonal periods, time-of-use windows that cross midnight, the plan's `LOCAL` or `AEST` time zone, stepped rates, and network and retail components. They update every minute without calling openfeed again. The Plan sensor now lists fees, discounts and incentives. They only appear when the plan includes tariff detail. Amber shares no plan, so Amber accounts get none.
- **Meter detail.** Openfeed's meter detail endpoint is now read once a day. New diagnostic sensors: Meter status, Meter read type, Jurisdiction, Service point classification, Generator, and NMI (disabled by default). Every meter detail field is also an attribute of Meter status. If the endpoint fails, the sensors fall back to the meters list.
- **Dashboard.** The sidebar dashboard adds a Plan tariff card when tariff sensors exist, and a Meter card.
- **Diagnostics.** Diagnostics now show whether tariff detail was shared, and the source of the meter detail.
