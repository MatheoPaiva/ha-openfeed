Easier to find: summary sensors, a bills calendar, a device link and a dashboard.

- Summary sensors: month-to-date and last 30 days grid import and export, last statement charges and credits (with net), and days of usage history held. Each lists the long-term statistic IDs it relates to.
- Bills calendar: one all-day event per invoice from issue date to due date, with amount, GST, balance at issue and payment status.
- Device link: the meter device's Visit link opens Energy settings.
- Dashboard: docs/dashboard.yaml, a ready-made view with monthly totals, daily energy, daily peaks, monthly billing, bills and the DER register.
- Nothing is added to the Energy dashboard automatically.
