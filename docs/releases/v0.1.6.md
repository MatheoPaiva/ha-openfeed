Fixes slow Home Assistant startup after updating to v0.1.5.

- v0.1.5 waited for the recorder while rebuilding payments and refunds. During startup the recorder does not finish its queue until startup completes, so Home Assistant waited until its startup timeout and the integration failed to load. Reloading the integration afterwards worked.
- v0.1.6 queues the delete and the rebuild without waiting. The recorder runs them in order, and the rebuilt series start from zero, so the result is the same.
- If v0.1.5 already loaded on your system, the rebuild has run and does not repeat.

Update needs a Home Assistant restart.
