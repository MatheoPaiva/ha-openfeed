First release.

- Config flow generates PS256 keys inside Home Assistant and shows the public JWKS to paste into your openfeed app registration.
- FAPI 2.0 sign-in: PAR, PKCE, private_key_jwt, DPoP-bound tokens, RFC 9207 issuer check, id_token verification.
- Disclosure consent deep link, with grant replace for reauth and reconfigure.
- Energy: account balance, latest invoice, plan, DER capacity and export limit, latest day consumption, export and controlled load.
- Hourly grid consumption, export and controlled load imported as external statistics for the Energy dashboard, with 60-day backfill and a rolling 7-day restatement.
- Optional banking balances.
- Revocation stops collection and purges cached data; credit exhaustion raises a repair issue.
- Redacted diagnostics.
